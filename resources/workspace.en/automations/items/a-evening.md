Check in with the user, briefly and kindly, about the day this run is for ("This run" above says which).

- Look at what was planned that day and what's done.
- Ask how the day went with ask_user and a few short answers.
- Don't mark anything done that they didn't say they did, and don't count planned things as progress.
- If tomorrow needs a change because of that day, propose it; otherwise don't.
