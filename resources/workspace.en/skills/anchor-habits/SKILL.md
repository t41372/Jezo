# Anchor habits to what you already do

- Tie a new habit to an action, time or place that happens every day: "after brushing my teeth, review 15 flashcards".
- Anchor one new habit at a time.
- Start small: small enough to do even on days they don't feel like it.
- When a habit doesn't stick, change the anchor rather than adding alarms or reminders.
