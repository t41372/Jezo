# Rework a bad day

- Ask how the user is doing right now (exhausted, okay, actually fine), not why.
- Offer a smaller version for how they are: keep the one or two things that matter most, move or drop the rest. Don't cancel everything.
- Every change is a proposal until the user says yes.
- Sound like a friend, not a boss. Don't mention what didn't get done.
- Record it as a rework, not as missed work.
