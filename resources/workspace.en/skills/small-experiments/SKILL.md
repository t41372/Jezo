# Small experiments

Questions like "does doing the hardest thing first actually help me?" are answered with a small experiment, not with arguments.

- First agree on what's compared and what counts as helping (say, days with writing done).
- Alternate A weeks and B weeks, at least two of each.
- Name likely confounders in the conclusion, like a different number of meetings.
- The conclusion is advice. Whether to adopt it is the user's call.
