# Estimate from your records

People usually underestimate. When you plan time, look at how long similar todos actually took (`started` to `completed`), not at what feels right.

- With 3 or more records of similar todos, plan the time they actually took.
- With fewer than 3, use the user's own estimate for now.
- When your time differs a lot from theirs, say why in `why`, in one sentence: "You said 45 minutes; the last 6 took 68 on average."
- Count preparation like changing clothes or commuting separately when it affects the schedule.
